# CommerceIQ Analysis Report

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 12,000 e-commerce transactions across 8 countries, 6 channels,
and 6 product categories reveals a healthy business with 32% gross margins
and significant customer retention opportunities.

## Key Findings

### Revenue Performance
- Total gross revenue: $3.67M across 12,000 orders
- Completion rate: 66.8% (8,015 completed, 1,980 refunded, 2,005 cancelled)
- Average order value: $307.09
- Average items per order: 2.52

### Customer Intelligence
- 1,725 of 2,000 customers (86.3%) have placed at least one order
- 336 customers classified as "Champions" generating 39% of revenue
- 213 customers at risk of churn with $380K historical revenue
- 468 customers classified as "Lost"

### Channel Performance
- Email channel has highest AOV ($321.52)
- Affiliate channel has lowest AOV
- Organic, paid_search, and social are primary acquisition channels

### Regional Analysis
- US leads revenue ($1.07M)
- 8 countries represented across the dataset
- Revenue distributed across multiple geographies

### Product Portfolio
- 6 categories with varying margin profiles
- Category-level margins range from ~30% to ~35%
- Top products drive disproportionate revenue

### Statistical Significance
- Kruskal-Wallis test shows significant AOV differences across channels
- Revenue distribution is NOT normally distributed (Shapiro-Wilk p < 0.05)
- Channel and country significantly affect order value

## Recommendations

### High Priority
1. **Re-engage At Risk customers** (213 customers, $380K historical revenue)
2. **Launch VIP program** for Champions (336 customers, 39% of revenue)

### Medium Priority
3. **Optimize channel spend** - shift toward email (highest AOV)
4. **Review product portfolio** - address low-margin products

### Low Priority
5. **Investigate geographic expansion** opportunities in underperforming markets

## Data Limitations

- Synthetic dataset: patterns are illustrative, not business-critical
- No discount/shipping data limits promotional analysis
- No customer demographics limits segmentation depth
- 12K orders may lack statistical power for small subgroups
